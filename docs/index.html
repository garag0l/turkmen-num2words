How it reads. A multiplier comes before its scale word (iki ýüz = 200, üç müň = 3,000). ýüz (100) and müň (1,000) drop the “bir” at one, while the borrowed million and milliard keep it.
Worked example from R. Gray’s grammar: 1,352,647 → bir million üç ýüz elli iki müň alty ýüz kyrk ýedi.
